NH Driver Quiz

A free app for studying all 100 "Test Your Knowledge" questions in the New Hampshire Driver's Manual. Learn the answers, practice, take timed tests, and play games that make it stick.

100 questions with explanations 40-question practice tests 3 study games Works offline
Recommended for your computer

Windows

Windows 10 or 11 · 64-bit
Download for Windows
NH-Driver-Quiz-Windows.zip · about 1.2 MB

How to install

  1. Click Download for Windows, then open your Downloads folder, right-click NH-Driver-Quiz-Windows.zip, and choose Extract All.
  2. Double-click NH Driver Quiz.exe. You can move it anywhere first, like your Desktop.
  3. If you see "Windows protected your PC," click More info, then Run anyway. This only happens the first time.
  4. To keep it handy, right-click the app and choose Pin to taskbar or Pin to Start.
The app uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which comes with Windows 10 and 11. If a PC is missing it, the app shows a link to the free download.
Recommended for your computer

Mac

macOS 12 Monterey or newer · Intel or Apple Silicon
Download for Mac
NH-Driver-Quiz-Mac.zip · about 125 KB (a small installer that builds the app)

How to install

  1. Click Download for Mac. Safari unzips it automatically. In Chrome or Firefox, open your Downloads folder and double-click NH-Driver-Quiz-Mac.zip.
  2. Open Terminal: press ⌘ Space, type Terminal, and press Return.
  3. Paste this line and press Return:
    bash ~/Downloads/install_nh_quiz.sh
  4. The app opens on its own and is saved to Applications. Drag it to your Dock to keep it there.
To update, download the newest installer and run the same line again. It replaces the old version.

How to use it

Pick a section from the sidebar on the left. A good order is Learn, then Practice, then Test.

📖

Learn

Read all 100 questions with the answer filled in and a short explanation of why.

  • Search by word or topic
  • Arrow keys move between questions
  • Filter to questions you've missed
✅

Practice

Answer one question at a time and find out right away if you're right.

  • Multiple Choice: press 1–4, then Enter
  • Open Note: type the answer, and suggestions appear as you type
  • Practice only your misses
📝

Test

40 random questions with no hints. You see your score and every answer at the end.

  • 80% (32 of 40) passes, same as the real NH test
  • Jump between questions before you finish
  • Retry just the ones you missed
🎮 Games, under "Games" in the sidebar. Hover over the ? next to each one for how to play.
🏎️

Lane Runner

Steer your car into the lane with the right answer before the gate. Use ← → or 1–4 to switch lanes. Crashes show the right answer and why.

🔢

Number Crunch

The number questions people miss most: feet, MPH, days, BAC. Miss one and you get a hot/cold hint and a second try.

🗺️

Road Trip

Drive across New Hampshire one town at a time. Pass each town's topic quiz with 80% to unlock the next, all the way to Test Day.

💾 Your progress is savedThe app remembers which questions you've missed and your game progress, even after you close it.
✍️ Typed answer marked wrong?Auto-checking looks for key words. If your answer was right in different words, click "I was right, count it."
